Before you email

Help us solve it quickly.

Please tell us what happened, what you expected to happen, your device model, iOS or Android version, and the Ace TMUA app version. Do not send your password or full payment details.

01

Purchases and Premium

Premium costs £5.99 per month in the UK; regional store pricing may vary. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. If Premium is not showing after payment, open the Premium screen and choose Restore purchases. If that does not work, contact us with the store receipt date and the email used for your Ace TMUA account.

02

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions must be cancelled through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting your Ace TMUA account does not automatically cancel a store subscription.

03

Account or password help

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen for an email reset link. For Apple or Google sign-in issues, check that you are using the same provider you originally selected.

04

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Account → Delete account and follow the confirmation steps. You can also email us from the address linked to your account for help with deletion.

05

Missing progress

Make sure you are signed into the same Ace TMUA account. Guest progress is stored on that device until you connect an account; signed-in progress can sync when an internet connection is available.

06

Question feedback

If you think a question or explanation needs attention, email the paper name, question number and a short description. We value precise feedback and will review it.
